# 🛒 Blinkit Grocery Product & Sales Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Business Problem & Overview
The primary goal of this project is to analyze the sales dynamics, customer preferences, and outlet performance for the **Blinkit** quick-commerce application. By transforming raw POS data into an interactive executive dashboard, this analysis provides actionable insights to optimize inventory management, marketing strategies, and overall business growth.

## 🛠️ Dashboard Features & Analytical Tasks Completed

1. **Fat Content Analysis:** Evaluates the breakdown of sales between *Low Fat* and *Regular* fat products[cite: 9].
2. **Item Type Distribution:** Visualizes sales performance across various grocery categories (Snack Foods, Fruits & Vegetables, Household, etc.)[cite: 9].
3. **Outlet Establishment Trend:** Tracks the expansion and sales growth of outlet establishments from 2012 to 2022[cite: 9].
4. **Outlet Size & Location Matrix:** Measures performance across store sizes (*Small, Medium, High*) and location tiers (*Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3*)[cite: 9].
5. **Outlet Type Comparison:** Comparative matrix across Grocery Stores and Supermarket Types (Type 1, Type 2, Type 3) evaluating Total Sales, Average Sales, Item Visibility, and Customer Ratings[cite: 9].
6. **Interactive Filter Panel:** Global slicers enabling deep-dive filtering by *Outlet Location Type*, *Outlet Size*, *Item Type*, and *Establishment Year*[cite: 9].
7. **Sync Slicers Implementation:** Synchronized interactive slicers across both dashboard pages for seamless multi-page cross-filtering.

---

## 💡 Key Business Takeaways & Inferences

1. **Dominance of Low-Fat Products:** Low Fat items drive over **64% of total sales revenue**, highlighting a strong consumer shift toward healthier product choices[cite: 9].
2. **Tier 3 Cities Drive Top Revenue:** **Tier 3 locations** generate the largest share of total revenue (~$472K+), proving that quick-commerce expansion in smaller cities yields higher market adoption[cite: 9].
3. **Supermarket Type 1 Efficiency:** **Supermarket Type 1** outlets generate the highest overall sales volume and number of transactions compared to smaller Grocery Stores[cite: 9].
4. **Top Categories:** **Fruits & Vegetables** and **Snack Foods** emerge as the top revenue-generating item types, contributing significantly to basket size[cite: 9].
5. **Impact of Store Size:** Medium-sized outlets achieve the optimal balance between high transaction volume and customer satisfaction ratings (~3.9 ★)[cite: 9].
